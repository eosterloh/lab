Once upon a time a little fox found a red ball and rolled it to his sister.
Lily dropped the cup so it broke and she felt sad. She told her mom.
Tom wanted to fly a kite. The wind was strong so the kite went high.
The cat sat on the mat and watched the bird until the bird flew away.
Mia shared her apples with her friends because sharing made them happy.
It started to rain so they ran inside and drank cocoa and read a book.
The puppy chased its tail in a circle until it got dizzy and sat down.
Grandpa planted seeds in the garden. In the spring green shoots came up.
Sam could not find his hat. It was under the bed next to a dusty shoe.
Anna baked bread. The kitchen smelled warm and everyone asked for a slice.
Ben said sorry after he bumped his sister. They built a tower of blocks.
The train went over the bridge and into the tunnel then out into the sun.
The dog waited by the door. When the girl came home it wagged its tail.
They made a snowman with a carrot nose. Later the sun made it small.
At night the town was quiet. Only the river made a soft sound by the mill.
A girl named June found a blue feather and put it in a book.
The horse drank from the stream and walked back to the barn.
He stacked the cups slower the second time and they stayed up.
The baby laughed when the dog licked her hand. Mom said be gentle.
Pia drew a house with a red roof. Her brother added a tree and a bird.
